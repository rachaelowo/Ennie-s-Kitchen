Meet Ennie — founder & chef

Hi, I'm Ennie.

To me, cooking has always been about people. I love being able to make something with my hands and have it bring happiness to someone else — whether it's feeding a family, helping someone celebrate, or giving someone a meal that reminds them of home.

Over the years I've been blessed to cook for families, celebrations, organizations, and professional athletes. But honestly, my favorite moments are when a customer says "Ennie, that food was so good," or that it reminded them of home.

Thank you for choosing Ennieskitchen and letting me be a small part of your table. I can't wait to cook for you.

Our mission

We don't believe in treating customers like order numbers. We take the time to understand your needs and prepare every meal with the same care we'd give our own family.

Our vision

To become a trusted name in Nigerian catering, known for authentic, home-style meals that bring people together at every occasion.
