Our story

"If there's one thing that has always been a part of my life, it's good food."

I was blessed to grow up surrounded by amazing women who loved to cook — both of my grandmothers, and my mom, an excellent cook too. I didn't learn from a cookbook. I learned by watching, tasting, and spending time in the kitchen with family.

Today, every meal we prepare is made fresh because that's the only way I know how to cook. Ennieskitchen is my way of sharing the flavors I grew up with and the traditions that shaped me.

— Ennie, Founder & Chef
